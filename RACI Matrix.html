What is a RACI Matrix?

A RACI matrix is a responsibility-assignment framework that clarifies who does what across an organization. For each key activity, every role receives one of four designations, eliminating ambiguity, preventing overlap, and ensuring nothing falls through the cracks. Only one role may be Accountable per activity; multiple roles may share Responsible.

R

Responsible

Does the work. Executes the task or activity. More than one Responsible role is permitted.

A

Accountable

Owns the outcome. Approves the work and is ultimately answerable. Only one per activity.

C

Consulted

Provides input before or during the work. Two-way communication; their feedback shapes decisions.

I

Informed

Kept in the loop after decisions are made. One-way communication; they need to know, not decide.
