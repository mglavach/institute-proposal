About Us

Mission

Westminster LEAD exists to develop and form teachers and students as image-bearers of God who cultivate wisdom, creativity, and stewardship through hands-on innovation, collaborative learning, and purposeful service that reflects Christ in the world.

Vision

A generation of teachers and students formed as ambassadors for Christ who think creatively, lead wisely, and serve faithfully, designing solutions and meeting real-world challenges with God’s wisdom and creativity.

Stability Statement

While tools, methods, and platforms will evolve, the purpose of education, to form students as ambassadors for Christ who create, communicate, lead, and serve with wisdom and integrity, remains unchanged. Emerging technologies are embraced as instruments for learning and expression, not as drivers of identity or purpose.

“ ”

Good things are already happening in classrooms across our school, from PK through
Elementary, Middle, and High School. This framework brings them together, giving
shared language and common purpose to the innovation already underway at every level.

The Gap

Creative projects, technology integration, and student-led learning exist across our school. But when innovation is left to individual teachers, it lives and dies with those individuals and never becomes a culture.

The Opportunity

Christian schools are navigating AI, shifting workforce expectations, and students who need more than content. They need formation. We have a moment to lead, not by chasing trends, but by anchoring innovation to a clear theological identity.

The Response

Westminster LEAD responds to this moment with five commitments:

  • Innovative student programming (PK–12)
  • Teacher development and cohort leadership
  • Technology Literacy and AI as a formation challenge
  • Signature showcases and real world application
  • A shared mission language across all four campuses

Our Name Mirrors Our Mission

Westminster LEAD is more than a name, it’s a commitment.

WESTMINSTER
Rooted in a Christ-centered legacy of academic excellence

Our school is a PK–12 community committed to forming students in truth, character, and Christian scholarship. Westminster LEAD inherits that legacy and gives it a coordinated, mission-rooted expression across every division.

LEAD
LEAD names both the acronym and the aim.
  • Learn: to grow in wisdom and understanding as image-bearers of God
  • Equip: to build the skills and creativity needed to design real solutions
  • Aspire: to pursue purposeful innovation that reflects God’s redemptive work
  • Disciple: to form and be formed as ambassadors for Christ in every classroom

Each letter reflects a piece of who we are becoming, together.

Areas of Exploration

  • Piloting approved platforms, curriculum and instructional workflows
  • Exploring ways new technology such as AI can support planning, differentiation, creativity, and productivity
  • Identifying appropriate student use cases and boundaries
  • Discussing challenges related to academic integrity and critical thinking
  • Sharing practical classroom examples and implementation reflections
  • Collaborating on best practices for responsible AI integration
  • Helping shape future professional learning opportunities and schoolwide guidance
“ ”

Westminster LEAD is not a new department, it’s a coordinating framework built on current staff and existing programs. Leadership roles are assigned, not added. The goal is coherence, not addition.

Guiding Principles

Technology should serve learning, not replace it.

  • Creativity and innovation
  • Critical thinking and discernment
  • Responsible leadership
  • Meaningful student engagement
  • Christ-centered learning and formation

Quarterly Collaboration Meetings

  • Sharing implementation experiences
  • Evaluating successes and challenges
  • Discussing emerging tools and trends
  • Reflecting on ethical considerations and instructional impact
  • Collaborating on recommendations for future implementation
