What is an Org Chart?

An organizational chart visually maps the structure and reporting relationships within an organization. It shows how roles connect: who reports to whom, how authority flows, and where each position sits within the broader hierarchy. For Westminster LEAD, the org chart reveals how leadership designations, campus directors, and innovation leads are structured across all divisions.

Hierarchy

Top-to-bottom flow from senior leadership down to campus and classroom roles.

Reporting Lines

Connecting lines show who each role formally reports to and receives direction from.

Role Clarity

Each node represents a distinct leadership designation, not a department, but a person with a purpose.

Span of Control

How many roles fall under each leader, showing the breadth of oversight and coordination.
