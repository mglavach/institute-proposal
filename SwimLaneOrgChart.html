Organizational Swim Lane

What is a Swim Lane Diagram?

A swim lane diagram organizes activities and responsibilities into horizontal bands, one per role or group. Unlike an org chart that shows structure, a swim lane shows process flow: what each role does across different stages of work, and where handoffs occur between them. Solid arrows show the direction of work; dashed arrows show results and reporting flowing back up.

Lanes

Each horizontal band represents a distinct role or organizational level in the process.

Process Flow

Vertical columns group related activities: strategy, operations, innovation programs, and reporting.

Handoffs

Arrows crossing lanes show where work passes from one role to the next as a task moves forward.

Direction

Solid lines show work flowing down; dashed lines show results and reporting flowing back up.
